## 2026-11-17 — v2: ECG, device form, cleanup, one design system (owner)

- **ECG with the sound (option A):** one exam records 20 s of sound **and** single-lead ECG at the same time; the
  doctor's exam page shows the ECG strip under the sound, the heart rate and the rhythm result. Rejected: a separate
  ECG exam (loses the sound/ECG timing) and a live ECG stream. ECG is doctor-only in v2 (patients and admin see none).
  A bad ECG never fails the exam; only the ECG part says "cannot judge".
- **Bench gate passed** on a real person: 70 /min, steady rhythm, clean QRS ~0.8 mV with gel electrodes RA under the
  right clavicle, LA on the left lower ribs (side), RL lower right abdomen, laptop on battery. Electrodes close together
  and horizontal (both under the clavicles) gave only ~0.1 mV. Which ADS channel carries the ECG depended on the cable
  and placement (CH2 in the owner's first log, CH1 today) → the device sends both channels, the server picks one.
- **Device form:** a chest strap like the owner's render; the microphone sits over the heart; the ECG uses an AUX jack
  on the device with **3 separate gel electrodes** (built-in electrodes were rejected: too close together).
- **ECG AI:** no ready, validated open model fits (licences, undocumented preprocessing), so we train a small model on
  PhysioNet/CinC 2017 (ODC-By) and test it once on a held-out part, plus an external check on selected PTB-XL lead II
  records (CC BY 4.0, ~50 MB of the 1.7 GB). Only "AF suspected / not" is shown, to the doctor, never as a diagnosis.
  No new runtime dependency (numpy + PyTorch).
- **AI weights stay in Git** (`demo/cardiophon/`), owner's choice; the licence note "use with citation, not
  redistribution" is a known risk the owner accepts.
- **One design system:** the redesign palette (petrol) wins, made phone-first; the old pages move to it and
  `.interface-design/system.md` is updated.
- **Medical wording:** invented "outside normal range" bands for vitals are removed (numbers only until a doctor sets
  ranges); a patient does not see the AI label of an exam where the doctor disagreed with the AI.
- **Board facts** (measured): ADS1292R ID 0x73 and streams fine; GPIO48 is both ADS PWDN and the on-board RGB LED, so
  the firmware never drives the LED; mic pins WS 6 / SD 5; only the left mic is fitted.
